# Napkin AI

> Napkin AI turns typed or pasted text into editable visuals — diagrams, charts, icons, and infographics — and into full presentations, with no prompting or design skill required.

Napkin has two products built on the same text-to-visual engine: Napkin Visuals (generate standalone diagrams, charts, and graphics from text) and Napkin Slides (beta — generate a complete, on-brand presentation deck). Both are used for docs, blog posts, social content, presentations, and internal communication. A developer API is also available for programmatic visual generation.

## Product

- [Napkin Visuals](https://www.napkin.ai/): Paste or type text and Napkin generates diagrams, charts, mind maps, and icons from it; fully editable colors, shapes, and layout; exports to PPT, PNG, PDF, or SVG.
- [Napkin Slides](https://www.napkin.ai/slides/): An AI presentation agent that asks about your goal, audience, and content before building anything — producing a tailored outline you approve, then a fully editable deck, rather than a single-shot generic template. Every element (layout, visuals, branding, copy) stays editable after generation. Exports to PPTX, Google Slides, PDF, PNG, or SVG. Currently in beta: usable today, still being refined.
